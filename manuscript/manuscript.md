# The changing burden of disease in Nigeria and West Africa, 1990–2023: DALYs, premature mortality, disability and uncertainty, with emphasis on neurological and vision disorders

**Author:** Ekemini Thompson

**Manuscript status:** Protocol / analysis plan. This is not a completed empirical research article.

## Abstract

See [`abstract.md`](abstract.md). The abstract is deliberately labelled protocol-stage because no verified GBD extracts have yet been incorporated.

## Introduction

Population health priorities change as populations grow, age and experience shifts in the distribution of causes of death and disability. National and regional estimates can help identify changes in premature mortality and non-fatal health loss, but interpretation requires attention to the underlying estimation framework, uncertainty and comparability across time. This study will describe the changing burden of disease in Nigeria and a defined West African comparator, with focused analyses of neurological disorders and vision-related conditions.

## Objectives

1. Describe changes in DALYs, YLLs and YLDs in Nigeria from 1990 to 2023.
2. Compare Nigeria with a clearly specified West African regional estimand.
3. Characterise neurological causes and vision-related impairment outcomes without double-counting hierarchy levels.
4. Examine differences by age and sex and distinguish counts from crude and age-standardised rates.
5. Quantify uncertainty using source-provided intervals and posterior draws where available.
6. Decompose changes in burden into population growth, age structure and age-specific rates where compatible inputs exist.

## Methods

The study will use the GBD 2023 release for 1990–2023. The GBD Results tool will be used to obtain estimates for Nigeria and the pre-specified comparator. The comparator must be explicitly defined; Western Sub-Saharan Africa will be used only if it matches the intended estimand. Cause and impairment categories will be mapped using the release-specific hierarchy IDs. Parent and child causes will not be summed together. Vision impairment will be distinguished from ophthalmic causes.

Primary outcomes are DALYs, YLLs and YLDs. Counts and rates will be analysed separately, as will crude and age-standardised rates. The standard population and source-defined units will be recorded. Descriptive endpoint changes and exploratory log-linear trends will be estimated. Trend regressions are not joinpoint models and will not be interpreted causally. Demographic decomposition will use age-specific population and rates at both endpoints and will be reported as an accounting decomposition, not causal attribution.

GBD uncertainty intervals will be reported as uncertainty intervals, not conventional confidence intervals. Where posterior draws are available and legally accessible, calculations will preserve draw identifiers and pairing. Marginal summary intervals will not be used to reconstruct joint uncertainty.

## Data management and reproducibility

All data extracts will be documented in the source manifest, including release, query filters, hierarchy IDs, extraction timestamp, units, age standard, uncertainty fields, access terms and SHA-256 checksum. Restricted source files will not be committed to the public repository. The harmonised analysis file will be frozen before final analysis.
