# Abstract (protocol stage)

**Background:** Nigeria and West Africa face changing patterns of communicable, non-communicable and injury-related health loss. Neurological disorders and vision-related conditions warrant focused assessment because they contribute to premature mortality and disability.

**Objective:** To describe changes in DALYs, YLLs and YLDs in Nigeria from 1990 to 2023, using Western Sub-Saharan Africa as a nested regional benchmark, with emphasis on neurological and vision-related outcomes.

**Methods:** This protocol specifies secondary analysis of GBD 2023 estimates. The primary neurological estimand is the GBD Level 2 neurological cause group; an expanded cross-hierarchy nervous-system grouping will be analysed separately. Blindness and vision loss will be analysed as an impairment outcome, separately from ophthalmic causes. Analyses will distinguish counts from crude and age-standardised rates, report sex and age strata, preserve uncertainty draws where available, and use demographic decomposition where compatible population inputs exist. The regional benchmark includes Nigeria and is therefore not an independent comparator.

**Results:** Not yet available. Official source extracts have not been incorporated or validated; no empirical estimates are presented in this repository.

**Conclusion:** The framework establishes a reproducible workflow. Conclusions will be written only after verified data acquisition and analysis.
