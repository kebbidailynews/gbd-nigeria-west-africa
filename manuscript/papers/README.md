# Manuscript papers

Two complementary manuscript frameworks are maintained in this folder.

1. [Paper 1 — The changing burden of disease in Nigeria and Western Sub-Saharan Africa, 1990–2023](paper_1_overall_disease_burden.md)  
   Focus: overall DALYs, YLLs, YLDs, premature mortality, disability, age-standardised trends, and regional benchmarking.

2. [Paper 2 — Neurological disorders and vision loss in Nigeria and Western Sub-Saharan Africa, 1990–2023](paper_2_neurological_vision_burden.md)  
   Focus: standard and expanded neurological estimands, blindness and vision loss as an impairment outcome, and separate ophthalmic causes.

Both manuscripts use GBD 2023 (1990–2023), share the project's uncertainty and reproducibility rules, and cite the relevant GBD and Nigeria/West Africa literature.
