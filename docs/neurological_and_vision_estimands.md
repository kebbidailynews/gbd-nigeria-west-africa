# Prespecified neurological and vision estimands

## Why two neurological groupings are needed

The basic GBD cause hierarchy's Level 2 neurological disorders group is narrower than the broader set of conditions that can affect the nervous system. Published GBD analyses of nervous-system health loss have used expanded groupings spanning multiple GBD cause branches. Those broader groupings must not be silently relabelled as the basic GBD neurological cause group.

### Primary neurological analysis

Use the official GBD 2023 Level 2 neurological disorders cause estimate as the primary aggregate. Use Level 3/4 causes to describe its composition. Preserve hierarchy IDs and do not add the Level 2 parent to child estimates.

### Expanded nervous-system analysis

A separately labelled secondary analysis may include conditions such as stroke, meningitis, encephalitis, tetanus, neonatal encephalopathy, nervous system cancers, neural tube defects and selected nervous-system injuries. The final list must be justified against GBD 2023 cause/sequela definitions and prespecified before results are examined. Conditions with only some neurological sequelae require a defensible method to isolate the neurological component; do not automatically attribute the full disease burden to neurological health loss.

## Vision analysis

- Impairment outcome: blindness and vision loss, selected in the impairment context.
- Underlying causes: refractive disorders, cataract, glaucoma, diabetic retinopathy and age-related macular degeneration, selected in the cause context.

Report impairment burden as the outcome of interest. Report cause-specific burden separately as the conditions contributing to vision loss. Do not sum the two categories.

## Core guardrails

1. Store source estimate context (cause, impairment, sequela, or other) with every record.
2. Retain source IDs, parent IDs and hierarchy levels.
3. Never use free-text fuzzy matching as the final classification method.
4. Keep aggregate parent estimates and detailed child estimates in separate result tables.
5. Publish the complete crosswalk as supplementary material with inclusion/exclusion rationale.
