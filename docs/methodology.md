# Methods framework

## Design

A descriptive population-level analysis of Global Burden of Disease estimates for Nigeria and a pre-specified West African comparator, 1990–2023 (GBD 2023 release). The analysis is not an independent re-estimation of GBD.

## Outcomes

Primary outcomes are DALYs, YLLs and YLDs. Report counts and rates separately, and crude and age-standardised rates separately. Use the exact GBD release definitions and standard population. Deaths, prevalence and incidence are secondary measures where relevant.

## Comparisons

Describe temporal changes and compare Nigeria with the explicitly defined regional estimand. Western Sub-Saharan Africa is not synonymous with a custom West Africa country grouping. Do not add regional totals to member-country totals.

## Focus conditions

Neurological causes and vision-related outcomes will be mapped against the selected release hierarchy. Distinguish vision impairment from underlying ophthalmic causes. Exclude overlapping parent/child causes from aggregate sums.

## Uncertainty

Use GBD-provided uncertainty intervals and posterior draws where lawfully available. Preserve draw IDs for draw-wise calculations. Do not infer joint uncertainty from marginal lower and upper bounds.

## Analysis

Summarise endpoint changes, exploratory log-linear trends and demographic decomposition. The log-linear regression is descriptive and is not joinpoint regression or causal inference. Decomposition requires age-specific populations and rates for both endpoints.

## Reporting

Follow GATHER for health estimates and applicable STROBE guidance. All methods, exclusions, data versions and transformations must be documented before results are interpreted.
