# Methods framework

## Design and estimand

A descriptive population-level analysis of Global Burden of Disease Study 2023 estimates for 1990–2023. This is not an independent re-estimation of GBD.

The primary national estimand is Nigeria. The regional benchmark is the GBD-defined Western Sub-Saharan Africa region. Nigeria is nested within that region; therefore, the regional comparison is descriptive and statistically non-independent. A secondary leave-Nigeria-out comparator may be constructed only after exact GBD 2023 country membership is verified. For this custom comparator, aggregate counts and denominators (or paired draws) correctly; never take an unweighted mean of country rates.

## Outcomes

Primary outcomes are DALYs, YLLs and YLDs. Report counts and rates separately, and crude and age-standardised rates separately. Use the GBD 2023 world standard population for age-standardised rates where supplied by the release. Do not combine different units or age standards in a single comparison.

Deaths, prevalence and incidence are secondary measures where relevant. YLLs represent years of life lost due to premature mortality; YLDs represent years lived in less than full health. DALYs are the sum of YLLs and YLDs.

## Neurological estimands

Report the basic GBD Level 2 neurological cause group separately from any expanded nervous-system grouping. The basic cause hierarchy contains a narrower set of causes. A broader nervous-system estimand can include conditions classified under other GBD branches (for example, stroke, meningitis, encephalitis, selected neonatal conditions, injuries and nervous system cancers), but this is a separate, literature-defined cross-hierarchy grouping. Prespecify its inclusion rules and avoid parent/child and cross-category double counting. Preserve GBD 2023 cause IDs and hierarchy levels.

## Vision estimands

Analyse blindness and vision loss as an impairment outcome using the GBD impairment estimate context. Analyse cataract, refractive disorders, glaucoma, diabetic retinopathy and age-related macular degeneration as ophthalmic causes in separate cause-context extracts. Do not add the impairment total to the cause-specific totals; they describe different estimands and may overlap.

## Comparisons and stratification

Describe temporal changes and compare Nigeria with the regional benchmark. Stratify by sex and age where supported by the source. Use the same year, metric, age group, sex, measure and release for each comparison. Regional rates should be sourced directly from GBD or reconstructed from compatible counts and population denominators; do not average country rates.

## Uncertainty

GBD 2023 reports estimates based on 250 draws, with 95% uncertainty intervals derived from the 2.5th and 97.5th percentiles of draws. Use source-provided interval estimates for descriptive reporting. Where draws are available and permitted, preserve original draw identifiers and pair them across calculations. Do not infer joint uncertainty from marginal lower and upper bounds, or independently re-number draws.

## Analysis

Calculate endpoint changes over prespecified common years (1990 and 2023), absolute and relative percentage change, and annualised percentage change. Exploratory log-linear trends may be presented as descriptive summaries, not as joinpoint regression or causal inference. Demographic decomposition requires compatible age-specific population and rates for both endpoints and is an accounting decomposition, not causal attribution.

## Reporting

Follow GATHER for health estimates and applicable STROBE guidance. Document data release, access date, filters, source files, hierarchy IDs, units, age standard, missingness, exclusions, validation and uncertainty handling before interpreting findings.
